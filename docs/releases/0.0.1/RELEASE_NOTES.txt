GearsVR 0.0.1

VR mod for Gears of War: Reloaded.
See README.txt for the short installation guide and folder layout.
Before installing: windowed/borderless mode, unlimited frame rate, DLSS/DLAA off; apply and exit the game.
Tested: Act 1, KRVR + Apple Vision Pro, VD + Quest 3.
Known issue: mounted machine-gun entry/exit may pause for about 2 seconds.
For bugs, use Export logs and send the ZIP with your headset, GPU and a description.

安装方法见 README.txt。安装前先在游戏里改为窗口化/无边框、帧率不限制、DLSS/DLAA关闭，保存后完全退出游戏。
将整包解压到游戏根目录，启动器与 Binaries_x64 文件夹并排。
