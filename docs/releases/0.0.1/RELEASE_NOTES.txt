GearsVR 0.0.1 — Release notes
2026-10-21

Hey Gears! GearsVR 0.0.1 is here!

This is the first public test release of my third-person VR mod for Gears of War: Reloaded.
I've played through Act 1 and tested these two OpenXR setups:
- KRVR + Apple Vision Pro
- Virtual Desktop + Meta Quest 3

I tested with in-game 4K, medium settings, and DLSS off.
If it feels choppy, try lowering Render scale in the launcher to 0.8 or 0.7.
VR eye resolution follows your OpenXR runtime's recommendation and Render scale;
the in-game 4K setting is for the desktop mirror, not 4K per eye.

Want the camera a little closer to the action? Try Camera closer. I like 0.4,
but see what feels right for you. Restart the game and VR after changing these settings.

A few things to know:
- Getting on or off a mounted machine gun may still pause for about 2 seconds.
- DLSS isn't compatible yet, so I've disabled it in the mod. Enabling it in the game menu won't turn it on.
- I've completed Act 1, but haven't tested all the later chapters yet. Let me know how you get on!

For setup, take a look at README.txt in the download.
If you find a bug, click Export logs in the launcher and send me the ZIP or post it on Discord.
Let me know your headset, GPU, and what happened. Screenshots or a video help too.

Thanks for playing and helping out, Gears. Have fun!


简体中文

Hey Gears！

GearsVR 的首个公开测试版本 0.0.1 来了！
现在可以在 VR 中以第三人称视角体验《战争机器：重装上阵》。

我已经亲自测试通过 Act 1（第一幕），并验证以下 OpenXR 组合可以游玩：
- KRVR + Apple Vision Pro
- Virtual Desktop（VD）+ Meta Quest 3

本次测试设置：游戏内 4K 分辨率、中等画质、DLSS 关闭。
实际流畅度取决于电脑配置、头显与串流设置；这里的 4K 不代表每眼 4K。

已知问题：
- 进入或退出固定机枪时，仍可能有约 2 秒延迟。
- DLSS 尚不兼容，已在底层禁用；在游戏菜单内开启也不会实际生效。
- 后续章节与更多游玩场景仍需验证。

如果觉得卡顿，可以把启动器中的 Render scale 调低一些，例如 0.8 或 0.7。
如果觉得镜头偏远、代入感不够，可以用 Camera closer 拉近镜头，我个人觉得 0.4 比较舒服。
修改这些参数后，请正常退出游戏，再重新启动游戏与 VR。

完整安装与启动步骤见同目录 README.txt。

发现 Bug 后，请通过启动器 Export logs / 导出日志保存诊断 ZIP，
附上问题描述，发送给我或发到 Discord。
非常感谢大家的体验与反馈！

谢谢各位 Gears，玩得开心！
